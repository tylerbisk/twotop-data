# twotop-data

The reviewed critic corpus the Two Top app downloads, so a weekly refresh reaches
phones without an App Store update.

Generated. Do not edit by hand: files here are copied from the app repository by
its publish workflow after a refresh is reviewed and merged. Each corpus sits
beside a manifest carrying its revision, SHA-256 and mention count, and the app
checks both before importing anything.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `critics/dc_critics.json` | Washington, DC critic mentions |
| `critics/dc_critics.manifest.json` | Its revision, hash and count |

Restaurant data derives from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).
Critic entries are quotes and links attributed to their publications.
